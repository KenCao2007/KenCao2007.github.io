About Me

I am a third-year Ph.D. student in Statistics at the University of Wisconsin–Madison, advised by Prof. Tengyang Xie. I received my B.S. in Statistics from Renmin University of China in 2024, where I worked with Prof. Feng Zhou.

I am looking for research internship positions for Summer 2027. Please feel free to reach out!

Besides research, I enjoy soccer and swimming. I also spent four wonderful years in my college Chinese orchestra.

Research

I am interested in how to learn a policy that acts in a very large action space over a long horizon. This includes continuous control in the physical world, and also LLMs. In particular, during my Ph.D., I have worked on:

  • Action tokenization for long-horizon control

  • Reinforcement learning for LLM post-training

  • Long-horizon reasoning with LLMs

In the past, I worked on deep probabilistic models, including point processes and approximate inference. I lead my projects end to end, from problem formulation and theory to implementation, experiments and writing.
